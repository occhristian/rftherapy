Practical details

Before your first session.

What should I bring?

Nothing special. You can simply arrive as you are. If there is something specific you want to discuss, you may make a few private notes beforehand.

How long are sessions?

Generally, a session is 1 hour long.
However, you might need to book multiple sessions, depending on your story, and the therapist's recommendation.

Where do sessions happen?

Sessions can hold online, via a video call, or face to face at our office in Awka, Anambra state.

What is the cancellation policy?

Any notice of cancellation should arrive at least 48hrs before the time of appointment.
There are no refunds for sessions that are cancelled otherwise.
