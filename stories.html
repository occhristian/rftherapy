Ethics of sharing

People before content.

Client stories should never be treated as marketing material first. Before publishing any story, RF Therapy should obtain clear, informed permission and agree exactly what may be shared.

Identifying details should be removed or altered where appropriate. The practitioner should also explain where and how the story will appear, and follow applicable professional and privacy obligations.
