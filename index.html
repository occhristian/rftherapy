About RF Therapy

Come as you are.

RF Therapy is built around a simple belief: meaningful change begins with a relationship where you feel safe enough to be honest.

Sessions are thoughtful, confidential and grounded in listening. We make room for the things that are difficult to name, while helping you explore patterns, relationships, emotions and decisions with compassion and clarity.

Relate and Flourish is more than a name. It is the direction of the work: connect more honestly, understand yourself more deeply, and grow into a fuller way of living.

Questions

Before you begin.

What happens in a first session?

The first session is primarily a conversation: what brought you in, what you hope will change, and what kind of support may be useful. You are not expected to arrive with a perfect explanation.

How confidential are sessions?

Confidentiality is a core part of the therapeutic relationship, subject to applicable professional, legal and safeguarding requirements. The exact policy should be confirmed by the practitioner.

Can I share a client story?

Client experiences should only be published after clear, informed consent, with the option to withdraw permission where applicable.

Can sessions happen online?

Online sessions can be offered where appropriate. The final site should specify availability, platform, fees and any location or licensing requirements that apply.

What if I am not sure therapy is for me?

You do not need to decide everything before reaching out. A first conversation can simply be a chance to understand the process and decide whether the fit feels right.
