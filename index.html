A place to begin

Come as you are.

RF Therapy is built around a simple belief: meaningful change begins with a relationship where you feel safe enough to be honest.

Sessions are thoughtful, confidential and grounded in listening. We make room for the things that are difficult to name, while helping you explore patterns, relationships, emotions and decisions with compassion and clarity.

Relate and Flourish is the direction of the work: connect more honestly, understand yourself more deeply, and grow into a fuller way of living.
