The work

A relationship first.

RF Therapy begins with the quality of the relationship. The goal is to create enough safety and trust for difficult thoughts, emotions and experiences to be explored without shame.

From there, sessions can help you notice patterns, understand what you need, make more intentional choices and develop healthier ways of relating to yourself and others.

Our principles

Quietly intentional.

01

Listen first

We make space for your story before rushing towards solutions.

02

Go at your pace

Meaningful work needs room. The process should respect readiness, boundaries and context.

03

Make insight useful

Reflection becomes valuable when it can change how you live, choose, communicate and relate.
